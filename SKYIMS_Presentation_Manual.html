SKYIMS: Sangguniang Kabataan Youth Information Management System

"Empowering Barangay Youth Through Modern Digital Governance, Real-Time Community Engagement, and Data-Driven Decision Making."

1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The Sangguniang Kabataan Youth Information Management System (SKYIMS) is an all-in-one digital platform created specifically for Barangay youth councils (Sangguniang Kabataan) and Katipunan ng Kabataan (KK) members.

In traditional barangay governance, youth profiling and event management rely heavily on manual paper sign-up sheets, physical logbooks, personal SMS blasting, and paper survey forms. This approach causes lost records, long queues during assemblies, inaccurate attendance counts, and delays in planning youth budgets.

SKYIMS solves these problems through two dedicated user experiences:

  • For Barangay Youth (Ages 15–30): Provides an instant digital Katipunan ng Kabataan membership card with an encrypted QR code, 1-click event RSVP, experience points (XP) and digital achievement badges for volunteerism, and mobile-friendly community surveys.
  • For SK Council Officials: Eliminates paper attendance logbooks using any smartphone camera as a contactless gate scanner, enables targeted SMS text broadcasts, maintains a verified master registry, and automatically produces live visual graphs for council budgeting and DILG compliance.

4. Detailed Plain-English Feature Manual

Feature 1: Online Youth Registration & Digital Profiling
A modern, mobile-friendly online portal allowing barangay youth to register their official Katipunan ng Kabataan membership from any smartphone or computer.
  • Personal Demographics: Captures full name, birthdate, gender, civil status, and contact phone number.
  • Purok & Sitio Mapping: Automatically categorizes residents into their respective zones (Purok 1 through 7, Sitio Crossing).
  • Youth Sector Classification: Records whether the youth is In-School, Out-of-School, Working, or with Specific Needs.
  • Voter Demographics: Tracks registered SK voters and National election voters for civic engagement monitoring.
  • ID Document Upload: Supports camera photo uploads or PDF documents of student IDs, government IDs, or Barangay Certificates up to 20MB.
Feature 2: ID Verification Queue (Fraud Prevention & Trust)
An administrative security room that ensures only legitimate barangay residents are verified into the Katipunan ng Kabataan master databank.
  • Instant Document Preview: SK officials can view uploaded ID photos and documents directly on screen.
  • 1-Click Approval: Officials can click "Verify" to approve the member or "Reject" with helpful feedback if an ID image is unreadable.
  • Verification Badges: Approved youth immediately receive a green verified seal on their digital card.
Feature 3: Digital KK Membership Card & Contactless QR Pass
A tamper-proof digital identification pass stored securely on the youth member's smartphone.
  • Unique Youth Code: Every youth is assigned an official lifelong code (e.g., TIBAO-2026-00001).
  • Interactive QR Code: Displays an encrypted QR code ready for instant scanning at any barangay entrance.
  • Offline Friendly: Youth can screenshot their digital pass or print it once; the camera scanner will read it just as fast.
Feature 4: Event & Program Management (NYC 9 Centers)
A centralized planning dashboard for scheduling youth seminars, athletic tournaments, tree plantings, and leadership summits.
  • Aligned with National Policy: Events are categorized under the 9 National Youth Commission (NYC) Centers of Participation (Governance, Health, Education, Climate Action, Sports, etc.).
  • Capacity Tracking: Tracks total slots, registered attendees, and walk-ins in real time.
  • 1-Click RSVP: Youth can view event mechanics and register with a single tap.
Feature 5: Smartphone Camera QR Scanner (Gate Admission)
Turns any official's smartphone camera into an ultra-fast, contactless barcode scanner at the Barangay Gym entrance.
  • Camera Support: Automatically connects to the phone's back camera with a 1-tap "Flip Camera" toggle for selfie cameras.
  • Audio & Vibration Feedback: Vibrates the phone and chimes on every scan so officials know attendance is logged without looking down.
  • Duplicate Check-In Protection: Instantly alerts officials if a youth attempts to scan twice, showing the exact time they already checked in.
  • Walk-In Friendly: If a youth's phone battery is drained, officials can type their phone number or name to log them in manually.
Feature 6: Targeted SMS Broadcast & Community Alert Dispatch
An emergency and announcement notification system delivering text messages directly to youth mobile phones.
  • Granular Audience Targeting: Broadcast to All KK Youth (General Assembly), Registered Attendees Only, or specific Purok/Sitio residents (e.g., river clean-up in Sitio Crossing).
  • Live Preview & 160-Char Counter: Shows exact SMS page segments and how the text appears on youth phones.
  • Complete Audit Log: Records recipient names, phone numbers, timestamps, and delivery status in a permanent database table.
  • Dual Operation: Runs in Simulator Mode for zero-cost demos, or connects to Semaphore to send live SMS across Globe, Smart, DITO, TM, and TNT.
Feature 7: Youth Gamification (XP Points, Levels & Badges)
A fun rewards system inspired by modern mobile apps that turns community service into an engaging achievement.
  • Instant XP Crediting: Youth earn experience points (+50 XP, +80 XP, +100 XP) the second their pass is scanned at an event.
  • Progression Tiers: Youth advance from Bronze Volunteer to Silver Civic Leader and Gold Youth Champion.
  • Digital Badges: Automatically unlocks special awards like "Eco Warrior" for tree planting or "First Responder" for disaster drills.
Feature 8: Real-Time Needs Assessment & Demographics Analytics
An interactive visual dashboard converting youth feedback and census data into clean charts for council budgeting.
  • Visual Demographics: Live charts showing youth distribution per Purok, education levels, and employment status.
  • Data-Driven Budgets: Gives SK officials hard data to defend their 10% Barangay Youth Budget before the Sangguniang Barangay.
  • 1-Click Excel/CSV Export: Exports official masterlists ready for submission to the DILG, Local Youth Development Office (LYDO), and Commission on Audit (COA).

6. Key Governance & Community Value

  • Republic Act 10742 (SK Reform Act) Mandate: Directly fulfills the legal requirement for SK councils to maintain a comprehensive, verified Katipunan ng Kabataan registry.
  • 100% Paperless & Cost Efficient: Eliminates thousands of pesos spent annually on printing paper sign-up sheets, survey questionnaires, and physical logbooks.
  • Ghost-Attendee Prevention: Digital QR scans record verified timestamps, ensuring transparency during Commission on Audit (COA) reviews for food packs, kits, and allowances.
  • Disaster Readiness: In emergency situations, the council can filter youth by Purok or Sitio and blast urgent SMS alerts within seconds.

7. Presentation Q&A Cheat Sheet (Common Panel Questions)

Q1: What if a youth doesn't have an internet connection at the gym?
Answer: The youth can save a screenshot of their Digital QR Pass or print a physical card once. The admin scanner reads offline images and paper passes instantly.

Q2: What if a youth's phone battery runs out?
Answer: The scanner interface includes a manual search bar where the official can type the youth's phone number or name to log them in with zero delay.

Q3: Does the SMS broadcast cost the barangay a lot of money?
Answer: No. The platform includes a free Simulator Mode for zero-cost testing and demonstrations, and connects to Philippine gateways like Semaphore at wholesale rates (approx. 50 centavos per text) for real barangay operations.

Q4: How is private youth data protected?
Answer: Youth members can only see their own passes and activities. Administrative access, ID documents, and masterlists are strictly restricted to authenticated SK Officials.
